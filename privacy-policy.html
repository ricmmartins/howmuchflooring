1. Information We Collect

HowMuchFlooring.com is a simple informational website and calculator tool. We do not require registration, do not offer user accounts, and do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, mailing address, or password in order for you to use the site.

When you visit the site, certain technical and usage data may be collected automatically by third-party services that help us operate and monetize the website. This may include:

  • Device and browser type
  • General geographic region
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website or traffic source
  • Approximate usage and engagement information
  • Advertising-related identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies managed by Google

The flooring calculations you enter into tools on the site are processed in your browser to display results. We do not create customer profiles or maintain user accounts tied to those calculator inputs.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use HowMuchFlooring.com and to improve the content and performance of the site. Our Google Analytics measurement ID is G-6CMBC8WTFD.

This information helps us understand which calculators and guides are most useful, identify technical problems, and improve the user experience. For more information about how Google handles data, visit Google's Privacy Policy.
